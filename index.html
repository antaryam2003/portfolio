About

I got to product the long way round.

I studied Mechanical Engineering at IIT Kanpur, and spent four years discovering that the part I liked best was never the machine — it was the question underneath it. Who is this actually for, and how would we know if it worked?

At Finlatics I segmented health & wellness (FMCG) and tech markets for an urban, digital-first audience, ran market tests against engagement, redemption and NPS to validate product fit, and sized TAM/SAM to back a go-to-market recommendation. In a design-and-innovation course at IITK I took Buknu Masala — an ODOP product made by self-help groups in Kanpur Dehat — and built a dual-channel expansion strategy around brand identity, retail-ready packaging and standardization.

The engineering half hasn’t gone anywhere. I’ve deployed a working DApp in Solidity, trained YOLO for real-time digit detection, and optimized robotic trajectories in MATLAB — enough to hold a real conversation with the people who build the thing, and to know what I’m asking of them.

Outside the work: I’ve led a 150-person show-management team at Techkriti’24, thrown shot put at the Inter-IIT Sports Meet, and mentored a 10-member team through trajectory optimization. I like problems where the constraint is real.
